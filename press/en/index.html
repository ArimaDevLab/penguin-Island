📝 About the game

On a small Antarctic island, Adélie penguins push blocks of ice from the quarry and build igloos, houses, a fish shop, ice towers, a park and a lighthouse, all on their own. As the town grows, they bridge over to the neighboring islands. The player does almost nothing but watch.

  • A town that grows while you watch: the penguins decide what to build and where. The town keeps growing while the game is closed (up to six hours)
  • A rookery with real penguin behavior: parents take turns on the egg, greet returning partners with an ecstatic call, chicks gather in a crèche, and one penguin keeps stealing pebbles from its neighbors
  • Lend a flipper, just a little: tap to toss a fish, or ask for a specific building in a specific spot
  • Favorite penguins: every penguin has a name, stats and a diary; pick a favorite and follow it around
  • Little moments: penguins napping on drifting ice floes, gulls circling overhead, and once in a while a humpback whale breaching
  • Photo mode and ambient sound: a photo mode that hides the UI and directs the camera for you, plus waves, calls and a music box synthesized live in the browser

📄 Press release

FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE Japanese solo developer releases Penguin Town 3D, a free browser game where you just watch penguins build a town Tokyo, Japan, November 20, 2026 – Solo developer ArimaDevLab has released Penguin Town 3D, a free 3D sandbox game that runs in any smartphone or PC browser. Players barely lift a finger: Adélie penguins haul blocks of ice, build homes and bridges, and grow their town on their own while the player watches. No download or sign-up is required. ABOUT THE GAME On a small Antarctic island, the penguins push blocks of ice from the quarry and stack them one by one into igloos, houses, a fish shop, ice towers, a park and a lighthouse. As the town grows, they build bridges to the neighboring islands. A day in the game lasts fifteen minutes, moving from daylight to sunset to aurora-lit nights. KEY FEATURES - A town that grows while you watch: the penguins decide what to build and where. The town keeps growing while the game is closed, for up to six hours - A rookery with real penguin behavior: on the next island, parents take turns keeping the egg warm, greet returning partners with an ecstatic call, and chicks gather in a crèche. One penguin keeps stealing pebbles from its neighbors' nests - Lend a flipper, just a little: tap to toss a fish, or request a specific building in a specific spot - Favorite penguins: every penguin has a name, stats (ice blocks hauled, trips down the slide) and a diary. Pick a favorite and follow it around - Little moments: penguins napping on drifting ice floes, gulls circling overhead, and once in a while a humpback whale breaching out at sea - Photo mode and ambient sound: a photo mode that hides the UI and directs the camera for you, plus waves, penguin and gull calls, and a music box BEHIND THE GAME The developer built the game in about a day, working in conversation with the AI assistant Claude by Anthropic. The developer's PC could not run 3D software, so the penguin and whale models were generated from Blender scripts in the cloud. The game uses no audio files: the waves and penguin calls are synthesized in real time in the browser. The whole game is a single HTML file of about 600 KB.
